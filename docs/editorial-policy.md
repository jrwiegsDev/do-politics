# Editorial policy

DoPolitics is openly progressive. This page says how the site writes about bills, so you can judge the work for yourself.

## Where the words come from

- **Official summaries.** Each bill shows the summaries written by the Congressional Research Service (CRS), the nonpartisan research office of the Library of Congress, exactly as published, with a link to the bill on Congress.gov.
- **Plain-language summaries and "How this does you."** These are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, from the CRS summary shown beside them. Every one is read and approved by DoPolitics before it appears, and each is labelled as generated.

## How we write about a bill

- We say plainly who a bill helps, who pays for it, and when. When a bill cuts benefits, shifts costs onto working families, or delays its harm until after an election, we say so. We do not soften harm to working and middle-class people.
- Every fact comes from the official record: what the bill does, who it affects, how much, and when. If the record does not say, we do not guess.
- We make reasonable inferences on how a bill or piece of legislation would affect lower-income, middle-class, and average Americans.

## Corrections

If something here is wrong, open an issue at https://github.com/jrwiegsDev/do-politics/issues. Corrections are made in the open.
