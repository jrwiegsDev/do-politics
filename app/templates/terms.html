What DoPolitics is

A free site that shows who represents an address in Congress, how they have voted, and what the bills they voted on do. DoPolitics is openly progressive, and our editorial policy says how we write about bills.

Check before you rely on it

  • The information comes from official sources: the Census Bureau, Congress.gov, the House Clerk, the Senate, and the public congress-legislators project. We update it regularly, but it can be out of date or wrong. Check the official source, linked throughout the site, before you rely on something.
  • Plain-language summaries are written by an AI model. A summary marked "Not yet reviewed by human eyes" has not been checked by a person yet. The official summary always appears below it.

Using the site

  • Don't flood it with automated requests or try to break it. The site limits how many lookups one visitor can make each minute, so it stays working for everyone.
